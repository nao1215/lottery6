All notable changes to this project are documented in this file. The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed

- The selfies in the chat are sharp: they were cut from 2K drawings and looked blurred; they are now enlarged first and are 1080x1440.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added

- A 6-of-43 number lottery played on a phone: pick six numbers or Random, buy a ticket for ¥200 and see the draw at once, with the odds and prizes of a real 6-of-43 lottery. Auto buy repeats the same numbers until you stop it.
- A gyaru plays it with her friend Aoi's money; when it runs out she asks him in a chat with a selfie, and he sends about ¥300,000 with a reply.
- A clock that moves three minutes a ticket, with the room's window turning from day to evening to night, and a save after every ticket.
- Japanese and English, starting in the browser's language, switched with the button at the top right of the phone.
- The browser version on GitHub Pages and desktop zips for Windows, macOS and Linux.
